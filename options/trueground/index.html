About

Micah Kleinert grew up on a dairy farm in West Branch, Michigan. Hard work was not a slogan. It was the day.

He ran his own small business in northern Michigan resort towns for more than 25 years. That stretch taught him how an idea becomes a product a customer will actually use.

Taking the propane and refined fuels division manager seat at Ellsworth Farmers Exchange felt like a return to those childhood and young-teen roots. Getting back to the country atmosphere he grew up in is soul-cleansing work. He is proud of the values that farm taught him and would not trade them — especially now, while raising his own children.

The covers are manufactured in the United States of America by Americans.
