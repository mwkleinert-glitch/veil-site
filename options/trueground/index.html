About

Micah Kleinert grew up on a dairy farm in West Branch, Michigan. Hard work was not a slogan. It was the day.

He operated his own small business in northern Michigan resort towns for more than 25 years. That work is how an idea becomes a finished product a customer can use.

The propane and refined fuels division manager role at Ellsworth Farmers Exchange is a return to those childhood and young-teen roots. The country atmosphere he grew up in is the same atmosphere he wants his own children to know. The values that farm taught him still hold.

Every cover is handmade in the USA using high-quality marine-grade Sunbrella and other canvas materials.
