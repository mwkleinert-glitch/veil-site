How it works

A wrap that comes off. A tank that stays a tank.

VEIL sits on the shell as visual and weather screening. It does not change the vessel, the setbacks, or the way propane is stored.

What VEIL is

  • Removable visual / weather screening for ASME aboveground LPG tanks
  • Light-value landscape patterns only
  • No weld and no drill — the tank is not modified
  • Service-friendly: dome flap and clear path to PRV, fill, gauge, and data plate
  • Retail finished Sleeve, a kit path, Magnet, and special-order Cast Film

What VEIL is not

  • Not insulating — and not sold as a heat jacket
  • Not a fire enclosure or a building
  • Not a pressure vessel and not a setback change
  • Not dark hunting camouflage
  • Not a substitute for required clearances, vegetation control, or leak checks

Why non-insulating matters

VEIL is designed to stay on the visual side of the line.

U.S. Patent 7,731,052 describes a camouflaging and insulating cover for compressed-gas tanks. VEIL is specified as non-insulating screening so it does not live in that claim space and so the tank can still exchange heat with outdoor air the way a working LPG vessel needs to.

2. Choose system + pattern

Sleeve is the default. Magnet for clean steel. Cast Film by special order. Six light-value patterns.

The wrap is cut and fastened so a driver can fill and a tech can read the plate without fighting hardware.
