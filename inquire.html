FAQ

Does this replace landscaping or a fence?

No. VEIL screens the tank itself. Plants still need proper setbacks. Fences and walls still cannot become a closed enclosure around an aboveground LPG tank.

Will it keep the tank warmer in winter?

That is not the job. VEIL is not insulation. A working tank needs to exchange heat with the air. We stay off that claim on purpose, including relative to U.S. Patent 7,731,052.

Can I pick a dark camo?

Not from this line. Light-value only: Birch Woodland, Limestone, Barn Silver, Prairie Grass, Winter Birch, Fieldstone.

Is VEIL patented?

Not an issued patent. A U.S. provisional application is on file: App. No. 64/158,376, filed September 19, 2026. Title: Removable camouflage cover system for aboveground liquefied-petroleum-gas storage tanks. First named inventor: Micah Woodrow Kleinert. The honest public line is Patent pending. Do not print “Patented.”

Who owns this?

“VEIL by EFE” is a trade style. Intellectual property stays with Micah Kleinert unless a separate written assignment says otherwise.

Are you the only one doing this?

Covers for tanks exist in the wider market. We are not aware of another Northern Michigan propane operator offering this kind of removable landscape wrap with service access designed in. That is the claim we will stand on until a neighbor operator proves otherwise.

What tanks can you wrap?

Aboveground ASME tanks in 120 V/H, 250, 325, 500, 850, and 1000 gallon. Hero SKU is 500.
