WHITEVEIL

The problem

A bright tank on a dark lot reads as an object, not as land.

Woods lots, lake edges, farmsteads, and HOA-facing yards all share the same complaint: the tank works, and it still looks like industrial hardware parked in the grass.

Paint is slow

Custom paint jobs fade, chip, and still leave a hard cylinder in the view. They also make inspection harder later.

Cages and walls fight service

Permanent screens become a delivery problem. Drivers need the dome, the fill, the gauge, and a clear walk-up.

Dark hunting camo is the wrong idea

VEIL ships light-value landscape patterns only. Dark wraps fight the culture of reflective tank finishes and are not in the line.

About

Micah Kleinert

Grew up on a dairy farm in West Branch, Michigan. Hard work was not a slogan. It was the day.

He ran his own small business in northern Michigan resort towns for more than 25 years. That stretch is how an idea becomes a product a customer will actually use.

Taking the propane and refined fuels division manager seat at Ellsworth Farmers Exchange felt like a return to those childhood and young-teen roots. Getting back to the country atmosphere he grew up in is soul-cleansing work. He is proud of the values that farm taught him and would not trade them — especially now, while raising his own children.

Every cover is handmade in the USA using high-quality marine-grade Sunbrella and other canvas materials.
