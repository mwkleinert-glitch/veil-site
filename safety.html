Safety & service

If the driver cannot fill it, it is not a VEIL install.

The wrap is cut so the working parts of the tank stay the working parts of the tank. Screening is the point. Blocking hardware is a fail.

Must stay clear

  • Pressure relief valve (PRV)
  • Fill connection and dome
  • Liquid-level gauge
  • Manufacturer data plate
  • Walk-up path for delivery and service

Will not do

  • No weld, no drill, no tapping the shell
  • No fire-rated enclosure claim
  • No insulation layer
  • No change to code setbacks
  • No covering of required markings

Local codes, the propane supplier, and the authority having jurisdiction still govern clearances and vegetation. VEIL does not replace a leak test, a regulator check, or an emergency plan. Illustrations on this site are conceptual; final cutouts follow the tank in front of us.
